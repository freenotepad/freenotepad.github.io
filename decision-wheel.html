    What is the Decision Wheel?

    A simple, visual way to let chance make a fair pick from your own list.

    A random wheel picker for any decision

    The Decision Wheel is a free spinner wheel online that turns any list of choices into a colorful, spinnable wheel. Type in your options — names, meals, movie titles, chores, prize tiers — and the wheel of names does the choosing for you, instantly and fairly.

    Built for speed, not sign-up forms

    There's no account to create and nothing to install. Open the page, edit the options, and spin. Your wheel options are saved automatically to your browser's local storage, so they're waiting for you next time you come back on the same device.

    Every option gets a fair, equal shot

    Each slice on the wheel is sized equally based on how many options you've added, and the spin animation lands on a genuinely random result using your browser's random number generator — no hidden weighting, no favorites.

    Useful for classrooms, teams, and game nights

    Teachers use it to call on students fairly, teams use it to pick who runs the next stand-up, and game nights use it as a prize wheel generator for parties and raffles. Turn on remove-after-spin mode to run a fair elimination draw.
