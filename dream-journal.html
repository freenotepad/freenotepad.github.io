What is the Dream Journal?

A dream journal online, ready the moment you wake up

The Dream Journal is a free dream log built for capturing dreams quickly, while they're still fresh. Set the mood, add a title, and write down what you remember — no app to download at 3am.

A dream diary that tracks lucid dreaming

Mark any entry as a lucid dream and the app keeps a running lucid dream tracker in your stats, so you can watch your lucid dreaming frequency change over time.

Tags help you spot dream signs

Recurring characters, places, or symbols are easy to lose track of. Tag each entry and browse your dream tracker app by theme to notice patterns across weeks or months.

Dream recall improves with consistent journaling. The streak counter shows how many days in a row you've recorded dreams, all saved locally with nothing to sync or install.
