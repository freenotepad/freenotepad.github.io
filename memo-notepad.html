Description

What is Memo Notepad?

A digital corkboard for the notes that don't need a whole document.

Sticky notes online, without the sticky mess

Memo Notepad is a free digital sticky-notes board built for quick, short-lived thoughts — a phone number, a reminder, an idea you don't want to forget. Click "New Memo" and start typing.

A quick notes app that colors itself organized

Give each memo one of five colors to group them visually — yellow for urgent, mint for ideas, whatever system works for you. The board re-sorts pinned notes to the top automatically.

Find any memo in seconds

As your board grows, the search bar filters by title and content instantly, so an online memo pad never turns into a pile you have to scroll through.

Every keystroke saved, nothing to install

This note-taking app runs entirely in your browser and autosaves to local storage as you type. There's no account, no sync delay, and no install — just open the page and write.
