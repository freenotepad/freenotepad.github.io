    Description

    What is the Study Planner?

    A lightweight way to organize study tasks and see your week at a glance.

    A study planner online, built for real workloads

    The Study Planner is a free study schedule maker for students who want a clear view of what's due, what's next, and where the time actually goes. Add tasks with a subject, due date, and priority, then check them off as you finish.

    A homework planner that keeps priorities visible

    Every task is tagged as low, medium, or high priority, so the things that matter most never get buried at the bottom of a long list. The stats bar keeps a running total of your progress as you complete tasks.

    A weekly study schedule you can build in minutes

    Switch to the schedule tab to lay out a weekly study schedule across seven days and eight time blocks. Click any slot to label it with a subject — a fast alternative to a printed timetable.

    Everything saved automatically, nothing to install

    This student task tracker runs entirely in your browser. Your tasks and schedule are saved to local storage automatically, so your exam planner is exactly where you left it next time you open the page.
